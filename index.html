02Computer vision · AI / ML

Face Mask Detection

Real-time detection that sorts every face into with mask, no mask or worn incorrectly, from a webcam, a video or a phone pointed at a crowd.

  • 98.97% test accuracy on 582 held-out faces with an EfficientNetV2-B0 classifier.
  • Temperature scaling cut calibration error from 0.067 to 0.010.
  • Far-range mode finds 89% of distant faces, up from 73%.

03Full stack

SeatSync

An event-ticket booking platform built around one guarantee: two people can never book the same seat.

  • Optimistic and pessimistic locking: 100 concurrent requests for one seat produce exactly one booking.
  • 12 REST APIs with JWT and role-based access, covered by 35 unit, integration and concurrency tests.
  • Load-tested with 500 concurrent users on 10,000 seats: zero double bookings.
